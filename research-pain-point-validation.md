## Summary

**Dunn directly addresses 8 of 12** documented small business pain points around invoicing and cash flow. It partially addresses 1 more (cash flow predictability — the ML engine will close this gap). It does not address paper-to-digital conversion (pain point 10), which is a separate problem solved by OCR/scanning tools.

The strongest validation: the top three pain points by severity (late invoices, manual chasing time, awkward follow-up) are Dunn's exact core features.
