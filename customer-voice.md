## Dunn's feature list (for the AppSumo page) — 10/9/26
Positioning: not "Dunn vs your invoicing app" but "your invoicing app **+** Dunn". Lead with automatic late fees.

- Automatic payment reminders on a proven schedule (before due, when late, final notice)
- **Automatic late fees**, flat or percent, with the grace period you choose
- A heads-up the day before any fee lands, so you can lower or waive it
- Lower or waive a fee even after it's added; the client gets an updated bill automatically
- Reminders pause the moment a client replies; the reply is forwarded to you
- Every email in your business's name, signed by you
- Recurring invoices: weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, or on a set day
- Get paid by card through your own Stripe; the money goes straight to you
- **"Who's always late":** lateness history for every client
- **"Who you waive fees for":** see the grace you give, and to whom
- Monthly revenue and late-fee reports, plus a CSV for QuickBooks, Xero or Wave
- A full timeline per invoice: every email, reply and fee, timestamped as proof
- Alerts for overdue invoices, payments, failed cards and bounced emails
- Cancel an invoice and tell the client in one click
- PDF download and a copyable payment link
- Free AI tools that work inside Claude and other assistants

## Competitor reviews — AppSumo (collected 10/9/26)

### "Auto-addition of late fees" — a user's #1 wishlist item (Invoiless/InvoiceQuick reviews)
Their full wishlist, in their order:
- **Auto-addition of late fees** ← Dunn's core feature
- Allowing clients to change quantities, select items/tasks, or choose packages in estimates
- Ability to add custom sections to estimates
- Connect bank accounts to automatically import income and expenses (rules like QuickBooks)
- Lower priority: convert to recurring invoice · block or enable partial payments · more customization

**Takeaway:** invoicing tools don't add late fees and their users want it. Marketing line idea: "Finally: automatic late fees."

### Purchase orders (InvoiceQuick review)
> "Before creating an invoice, I need to release a purchase order against a pro forma invoice generated by the supplier. Is it possible to create a purchase order first and then convert it to an invoice?"

**Takeaway:** procurement isn't Dunn's job. But big clients often won't pay without their **PO number on the invoice** → optional PO number field (post-launch list).

### Item selector bug + no support reply (InvoiceQuick, 1 star)
> "…the item selector does not provide a full list of items and services… I have raised numerous support queries and have yet to get a response… I will be requesting a refund."

**Takeaway:** on AppSumo, slow support costs more than the bug. Answer within a day during the launch. Some buyers will want saved services / line items (Dunn: one amount per invoice today).

### Other complaints seen
- "No way to send email through your own business address (SMTP)" (InvoiceQuick) → Dunn sends as "Business via Dunn"; custom sending domain = possible later upgrade.
- "Reporting is basic" / cash-basis only (Invoiless) → Dunn's reports + "Who you waive fees for" are a strength; lead with them.
