Questions

What owners ask first.

Do I need a Stripe account?

Yes. Dunn creates your invoices in your own Stripe account, and your clients pay through Stripe by card or bank transfer. The money goes straight to you. Dunn never holds it.

Will my clients know I use Dunn?

Emails go out in your business’s name and sign off with your name. Replies come back to you.

How many emails will my client get?

The invoice, one friendly reminder 4 days before it’s due, then a notice or two if it goes late. If they reply, the reminders stop.

When does the late fee apply?

You decide: the day after the due date, or after a grace period you pick. The fee is written on the invoice, and you can approve, lower or waive it.

What happens when a client replies?

Dunn pauses the reminders and sends the reply to you. Hit Reply to answer them directly. The reply is kept with the invoice as your record.

What counts as a client on the $39 plan?

Each client you invoice in a month, up to 5, billed as often as weekly. Need more? Unlimited is $59.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pay monthly or yearly, and cancel from Settings whenever you like. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
