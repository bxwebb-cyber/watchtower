# Dunn — AppSumo Lifetime Deal Go-Plan

Product: Dunn (getdunn.org) — automatic invoice reminders & late fees.
Goal of this launch: VOLUME of buyers (cash + validation + testers + reviews),
not price-perception preservation. This is a one-time launch push.

## Current Dunn pricing (getdunn.org, live)
| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Solo (up to 5 clients, billed as often as weekly) | $39 | $390 ($32.50/mo) |
| Unlimited | $59 | $540 ($45/mo) |

Both yearly options = "2 months free."

## Locked LTD pricing (volume-optimized — AppSumo sweet spot)
| Tier | LTD price | vs monthly | vs annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Lifetime Solo (5 clients) | $69 one-time | 1.8x | ~15% of $468 |
| Tier 2 — Lifetime Unlimited | $149 one-time | 2.5x | ~21% of $708 |

Why: entry tier in AppSumo's $59–79 convert zone drives impulse buys; the
Solo→Unlimited jump ($69→$149) preserves an upsell path inside one deal and
mirrors the $39/$59 plan structure.

## Lifetime language (protects you — must include)
- "Lifetime" = for the life of the product (as long as Dunn keeps running),
  not a perpetual commitment if the product ever shuts down.
- Fair-use ceiling on Tier 2 unlimited email volume. Dunn's whole job is
  sending reminders; unbounded lifetime email = unbounded Resend cost. State a
  sane fair-use volume and note heavy use may need an add-on.
- Anchor the LTD against YEARLY price, not monthly, so the framing stays fair.

## How AppSumo works (timeline)
1. LAUNCH DUNN CLEAN first — real user runs a real invoice through
   (Stripe connect → invoice → reminders → late fee → payment) before
   applying. No broken first impressions: AppSumo buyers review publicly.
2. Apply to AppSumo once working. AppSumo curates and QUEUES you — the gap
   between acceptance and going live can be weeks to months. Start the
   application early once Dunn is demonstrably working.
3. Live deal run: ~30 days. Buyers can purchase only during this window.
4. THE BURST IS DAY 1–2: most sales land in the first 24–48 hours when the
   deal hits AppSumo's email list, then taper through the ~30 days. Make the
   redemption smooth (code → Dunn account → Stripe connect) so the burst is a
   good experience and generates reviews.
5. Commission: AppSumo takes a cut per sale (historically ~half, split varies
   by deal/terms — CONFIRM the exact current split when accepted).
6. Payouts: batched after the deal closes, not real-time per sale.
7. No stacking (AppSumo moved away from it) — the two tiers do that job.
8. When it's done, it's done: deal page shows "deal ended," AppSumo's list
   rarely re-promotes. Treat as a one-time event.

## Launch sequence
1. Dunn fully working with at least 1–2 real clients/invoices (release to
   production clean).
2. Write the AppSumo application + asset pack (logo, screenshots, demo).
3. On acceptance + slot assignment, prep redemption flow + support plan.
4. Day 1: monitoring + fast support during the burst.
5. Post-deal: harvest reviews, convert engaged LTD buyers toward the paid
   monthly/annual plans where possible.
