## One-liner (short)
Dunn sends invoice reminders and adds late fees automatically, so you get paid without chasing clients.

## The problem
Getting paid is a second job for freelancers and small businesses. Invoices go out, then sit. Owners send awkward "just checking in" emails, avoid charging the late fee they agreed on because it feels confrontational, and lose track of who always pays late. 47% of small businesses owed money have invoices 30+ days late (QuickBooks 2025 Late Payments Report).

## What Dunn does
Dunn connects to the business's own Stripe account and runs the whole "get paid" process:
- Sends the invoice and a polite reminder before it's due, then follow-ups if it goes late, in the owner's name.
- **Adds the late fee automatically** when the grace period passes, with a heads-up to the owner the day before so they can lower or waive it. (Invoicing apps don't do this; users ask for it in reviews.)
- Pauses reminders the moment a client replies and forwards the reply to the owner.
- Lets clients pay by card or bank transfer (ACH, ~0.8% capped at $5) — money goes straight to the owner's Stripe; Dunn never holds it.
- Shows who's always late, who pays on time, and which clients the owner keeps waiving fees for.

## Who it's for
Freelancers, consultants, agencies, studios and service businesses who invoice clients (designers, developers, photographers, contractors, coaches, bookkeepers).

## Key features (for the listing)
- Automatic payment reminders on a proven schedule (before due, when late, final notice)
- **Automatic late fees** — flat or percent, grace period you choose
- Heads-up the day before any fee; lower or waive with one click (even after it's added)
- Reminders stop when a client replies; replies forwarded to you
- Line items + saved services (pick from your list, no retyping)
- Recurring invoices: weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, or a set day
- Card and bank transfer (ACH) through your own Stripe
- PO numbers on invoices
- "Who's always late" + "Who you waive fees for" reports, revenue reports, CSV for QuickBooks/Xero/Wave
- A timestamped history of every email, reply and fee on each invoice
- Alerts for overdue invoices, payments, failed payments and bounced emails
- Free AI tools for Claude and other assistants (due dates, late-fee math, reminder emails)

## How it's different
Invoicing apps (Invoiless, FreshBooks, Wave, InvoiceQuick) help you *send* invoices. Dunn is built to get them *paid*: the reminder schedule, the automatic late fee with a heads-up, reply detection, and the lateness history are the product, not an add-on. Positioning: "Invoicing that actually gets you paid."
